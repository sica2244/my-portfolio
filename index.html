Basic System & Photogates System

Design → VR asset

Problem. A physics track for kinematics/dynamics experiments — students reposition the photogates, end stops, and pulley for each run; the VR version needed that same freedom.

Built. Modeled as a 22-body assembly — track, cart, pulley, photogate, fence, rod, elastic bumper, and end stop all kept independent, exported as STL, prepped in Maya, painted in Substance Painter, and staged in Unreal as separately placeable parts.
