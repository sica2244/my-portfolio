Project overview

ProblemA physics track for kinematics/dynamics experiments — students reposition the photogates, end stops, and pulley for each run; the VR version needed that same freedom.
BuiltModeled as a 22-body assembly, with each part kept independent and exported as a separate STL for VR placement and interaction.

Process

01 — SolidWorks

SolidWorks is used to model and identify the movable parts, then export them separately as STL files so each component keeps its own functional logic in VR.

02 — Maya

The individual parts are prepared in Maya to optimize their geometry and export them in a file format ready for final presentation.

03 — Substance + Unreal

The FBX parts are painted in Substance Painter and then imported with their textures into Unreal Engine for final assembly and interaction.
