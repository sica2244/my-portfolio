Project overview

ProblemInstrument housing with a control panel and motion-based parts requiring a clear, interactive layout for VR demonstration.
BuiltModeled in SolidWorks, then exported as separate STL components for clean preparation and interaction in the VR scene.

Process

01 — SolidWorks

SolidWorks is used to model the instrument and identify the movable parts, then export them separately as STL files to keep each component functional.

02 — Maya

Those parts are prepared in Maya, cleaned up, and exported as FBX files so they can be passed into the material and scene workflow.

03 — Substance + Unreal

The FBX models are painted in Substance Painter and imported with their textures into Unreal Engine to complete the final VR environment.
