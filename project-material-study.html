Project overview

ProblemIndustrial pipe machining assembly with multiple mechanical components and complex geometry that needed to be accurately reproduced in 3D.
BuiltModeled as a 22-part movable assembly, with individual components prepared for interaction and animation. Parts were exported in STL and FBX formats for use in 3D and VR environments.

Process

01 — SolidWorks

SolidWorks is used to model the industrial pipe machining assembly and define the individual moving components for accurate 3D reproduction.

02 — Maya

The assembly is prepared in Maya, with part hierarchy and geometry cleaned up for export and use in a real-time environment.

03 — Substance + Unreal

The FBX parts are textured and assembled in Substance and Unreal for final presentation, interaction, and animation in the VR workflow.
